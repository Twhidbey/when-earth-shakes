## Threads in the story
- The dream (beat 4) comes true in beats 34 to 35: the giant shadow, the field on fire (04 matches 36).
- The grandfather "lived through a great fire": the armour, the crushed helmet and the sword in the reservoir may be from an earlier fight with the Beast.
- Red throughout: the blanket, the sneaker, the seismometer, the fire.
- His book's title is the film's title.
