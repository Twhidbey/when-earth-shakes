# When Earth Shakes: story beats (DRAFT, read from the stills; to be corrected)

Iowa, 1930. A seismologist comes to a farming town where the ground has been shaking,
and finds a black dragon under the fields. Audience: young adults, clean enough for a 5-year-old.

1. **Arrival.** Over the fields (47). A lone car on the plain (10). Wheat, a hand brushing it (05).
2. **Investigation.** Records and papers (08), the bookshop (07), the red-haired librarian (09).
   In the field the red box picks something up underground (11).
3. **Signs.** Smoke from the field (12); the town watches (15); a burnt trail to the houses (17);
   a lost red sneaker (03); a boy awake at night (02); the boy and his mother in the kitchen (06).
4. **First encounter, defeat.** The eye (28), the scales (27). He is hurt (16).
5. **The town's secret.** People in white crowd a dark doorway (18). The woman waits (23),
   works the red cloth (22), walks the burnt field (13), looks at us from the wheat (14).
6. **Resolve.** Bandaged stare (19). A handshake: an ally (24). The shotgun (21); shotgun or sword, or both (40, 42, 43). The helmet (20).
7. **The hunt.** Helmeted through the wheat (25, 26), aiming across the field (35),
   running at the wall of fire (36), striding through burning wheat (37).
8. **The fight.** On the rock heap under the dragon (31, 32, 33, 34). In the water as fire blooms (38).
   A sword (40, 42): the final face-off under the spread wings (43).
9. **The dragon leaves.** It rises into the sun (45), leaving a trail (46); she watches it go (48, 44).
10. **After.** The helmet in still water (39); wading into the sunset pool (41); alone on the rocks (30). The boy before the fire (04).

Open questions: who the boy is; the woman's role; the people in white; how the dragon is ended
(45/46 suggest it is driven back into the sky, not killed); 1930 vs the 1950s look of 01, 03, 06, 10; the shotgun man's face (21); shotgun or sword, or both (40, 42, 43).
