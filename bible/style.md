# Style card (draft): painted American realism, in the manner of Bo Bartlett

Described in words only: the owner's example paintings are a living artist's copyrighted work, so they are not
given to any image model. The models get these words plus the project's own stills as references.

- **Open land, low horizon, huge sky.** Half the frame or more is sky, often one smooth gradient; the land a single band.
- **Clear, low, raking light.** Early morning or evening; long soft shadows; at night one strong light source (a beam, a fire).
- **Smooth, polished oil paint.** Fine, quiet brushwork; never loose or chaotic; soft edges in the distance.
- **Figures large, still and posed.** Often seen from behind, in profile, or staring straight out; a frozen moment just before or after the event.
- **One strange event in an ordinary scene.** A field on fire, a beam of light, a crash, smoke on the horizon.
- **Muted earth colours with one saturated accent,** usually red (the seismometer, the barrel, a cloak), sometimes the white of a dress or shirt.
- **Faces calm and serious, never acting.** Emotion comes from the situation and the pose.
- **Wind as the motion cue:** hair, coat tails, dresses and grass all blown one way.
- **Composition:** a few figures, spaced apart, standing on one line (a road, a ridge, a field edge); much empty space.

Shots the examples suggest for this story:
- a man carrying another in his arms toward us (beat 15: carrying the Bookkeeper to the farmhouse)
- a beam or column of light rising from the dark plain, small figures beside it (the crater, the end)
- the Lady in her white dress standing on the road, wind in her hair, smoke on the horizon behind
- a figure aiming a shotgun, side on, on an empty road or ridge (beat 32)

## Body proportions
Slender and realistically proportioned, as in American realist painting: long legs, narrow shoulders, long limbs, about 8 heads tall.
For the cards only, cropped bodies (shoulders down, no faces) from the owner's example paintings were given to the
image model as a proportion reference, at the owner's request. They are not stored in the repo and are not reproduced.
