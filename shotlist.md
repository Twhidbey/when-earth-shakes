# When Earth Shakes: Midjourney shot list

One shot per story beat (beat numbers from `story.md`), in story order. The owner runs these in Midjourney; Claude does
canon fixes, animation (Modal), the cut and the sound.

## How to use
- **Every prompt ends with the style tail** below. Paste it as written, or save it in Midjourney as a preset.
- **Style reference (`--sref`), the same for every shot:** stills `04-boy-fire-field`, `11-strange_sounds2`, `44-woman-red-field`
  (plus any Bo Bartlett pictures you choose to add; your call).
- **Character / object references** (`--cref`, or omni-reference `--oref` on newer versions), by code:
  - **BK** = the Bookkeeper's face and clothes: `bible/cards/bookkeeper.jpg`
  - **SUIT** = the Bookkeeper in helmet, coat and gloves: `bible/sample-scene.jpg`
  - **LADY** = her original face: `stills/14-found_in_burning_field4.png` (and `bible/cards/lady.jpg` for the dress)
  - **BOY** = `bible/cards/boy.jpg`
  - **PROPS** = `bible/cards/props.jpg` (coat, shotgun, straight sword, red seismometer)
- **Canon reminders** (also written into each prompt): helmet = narrow pale grey steel shell, WIDE letterbox visor, dark cloth hood;
  overcoat rust-brown, ALWAYS buttoned to the throat, collar up; thin brown leather gloves in the suit; straight old sword;
  1931 for the main story, about 1910 for the boy. The Beast is NEVER seen whole in the trailer: shadows, smoke, scale, fire.
- **Staging rules** (the Bo Bartlett shock): low horizon, huge calm sky, figures large and close or tiny and far (never mid),
  off-centre, still, calm faces, one strange thing treated matter-of-factly.
- Upload picks to `stills/` named by shot number, e.g. `S07-seismometer.png`.

**Style tail:**
`contemporary American realist oil painting, smooth matte paint, clear low raking light, muted earth colours with one strong colour, figures utterly still, plain real faces, no fantasy --ar 16:9 --style raw --s 150`

Priority: ★ = trailer shot (do these first).

## Part 1: the boy (about 1910)

**S01 ★ The usual house** (beat 1) · refs BOY
> about 1910, a small white clapboard farmhouse on a flat Iowa plain under a huge pale morning sky, very low horizon, a boy of nine in a pale short-sleeved shirt and long grey trousers stands seen from behind on the dirt path, small, off-centre, laundry still on a line, [style tail]

**S02 Unusual dreams** (beat 2) · refs BOY

**S03 The lost shoe** (beat 3) · no refs
> about 1910, the dark gap beneath a wooden back porch, one small worn leather boot lying in the dust in a single shaft of morning light, the rest in deep shadow, low camera close to the ground, [style tail]

**S04 ★ The dream of the field** (beat 4) · refs BOY

**S05 He knew it was wheat** (beat 5) · refs BOY
